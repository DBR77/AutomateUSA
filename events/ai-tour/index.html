Bring your factory problem

Submit a challenge before the workshop.

Write it or record it later when the connected version launches. You control how the case may be used.

A useful challenge includes

1

Current process

What happens today and where the difficulty appears.

2

Business impact

Time, cost, capacity, quality, safety or delivery effect.

3

Desired result

What would materially improve and how you would recognize it.

Privacy first.Do not include personal data, passwords, export-controlled information, customer secrets or restricted drawings in the initial submission.

You choose the visibility

Every submitted challenge receives one explicit classification.

Open workshop case

May be discussed with your company identified.

Anonymous workshop case

May be presented after company-identifying details are removed.

Confidential DBR77 review

Restricted to authorized DBR77 personnel for private follow-up.
