FAQ

Everything you need before registering.

Is IEX 2026 free?

Yes. Online access, live questions, recordings and the resource package are free after registration.

Is IEX online or in person?

It is an online-first global broadcast produced from a professional studio in Charlotte. A small physical audience participates by invitation.

Can I attend only one session?

Yes. Registration covers the full day and you may join the sessions most relevant to you.

Will recordings be available?

Registered participants receive approved recordings of all five conversations.

How do I receive the Physical AI report?

An executive preview follows registration. The full report premieres during the opening broadcast.

Can I submit a factory challenge?

Yes. The confirmation journey will include written, audio, anonymous and confidential submission options.

Can international participants attend?

Yes. IEX is designed as a global manufacturing broadcast with Eastern Time as the official schedule.

Are the speakers confirmed?

Speaker and partner announcements will be published only after approval. Invited experts are never presented as confirmed speakers.
